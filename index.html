Employee & Role Permissions

Demonstration of role-based access control (RBAC) across retail modules

Demonstration Disclaimer: Client-side role restrictions in this single-page application are for presentation and workflow simulation only. In an enterprise production deployment, role enforcement is performed securely via server-side session authentication and JWT tokens.
Full Privileges

Administrator

Store owner or general manager with unrestricted access to every feature, master data, settings, and reports.

  • Full POS Billing & Cart Management
  • Product CRUD & Stock Adjustments
  • Suppliers & Receiving Purchase Orders
  • Customer Loyalty Master Records
  • Financial Sales & Margin Analytics
  • Reset & Backup LocalStorage Data
Store Operations

Store Manager

Supervises inventory, supplier purchase orders, sales performance, and resolves customer loyalty issues.

  • Full POS Billing & Cart Management
  • Product CRUD & Stock Adjustments
  • Suppliers & Purchase Orders
  • View Sales & Financial Reports
  • System Reset / Data Purge Restricted
Front Desk Checkout

Cashier

Responsible for scanning items, taking customer tenders, generating receipts, and registering customer loyalty.

  • Full POS Billing & Receipt Printing
  • Register New Customer & Redeem Points
  • Cannot Add/Edit Products or Cost Price
  • Cannot Modify Suppliers or Purchase Orders
  • Executive Analytics Restricted
Warehouse & Receiving

Inventory Staff

Monitors warehouse stock, records physical damages/breakages, and receives replenishment purchase orders.

  • Product Inventory & Safety Thresholds
  • Stock Movement Adjustments & Logs
  • Receive Goods Against Purchase Orders
  • POS Billing Restricted
  • Financial Margin Analytics Restricted
